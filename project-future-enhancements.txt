FUTURE ENHANCEMENTS

Writer can be expanded over time to support evolving individual and team knowledge-work needs. Potential future enhancements include:

1. Improved Knowledge Management
• Add complete note version history with the ability to compare and restore earlier versions.
• Introduce templates, richer formatting, and customizable workspace layouts.
• Support exporting notes and workspace content to formats such as PDF and Markdown.

2. Productivity and Integrations
• Add tasks, reminders, and notifications for assignments, mentions, and replies.
• Integrate with calendars, cloud storage, and other productivity tools.
• Provide an activity feed and audit history to help teams follow changes across workspaces.

3. Smarter Search and AI Assistance
• Improve search with advanced filters and semantic matching across workspace content.
• Explore AI-assisted summaries, content recommendations, and suggestions for organizing knowledge.

4. Mobile Access and Offline Support
• Develop mobile applications for iOS and Android to make workspace content easier to access on the go.
• Consider secure offline access with synchronization when an internet connection is available.

5. Security and Insights
• Strengthen account protection with options such as two-factor authentication.
• Expand workspace activity analytics to help teams understand how their knowledge resources are used.

These enhancements are possibilities for future development and are not part of the current project scope.
